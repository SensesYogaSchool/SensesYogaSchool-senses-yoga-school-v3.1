Participant Safety & Community Care

Shared expectations for consent, accessibility, responsible practice, and care across Senses Yoga School programs.

Working draft · November 2026
Important: This page describes general safety expectations. It is not a medical screening form, legal liability waiver, emergency plan, or substitute for program-specific consent. Each host location and program must maintain procedures appropriate to its activities.

1. Participation and personal responsibility

Participation in movement, yoga, breathwork, meditation, outdoor activities, and other educational experiences is voluntary. Choose an appropriate level of participation, take breaks, and stop any activity that causes pain, dizziness, distress, or unusual discomfort. You may decline an exercise or request an alternative without needing to explain publicly.

2. Health considerations

Activities may involve physical movement, changes in breathing, balance, sensory attention, or outdoor conditions. If you have a health concern, injury, are pregnant, or are unsure whether an activity is appropriate, consult a qualified healthcare professional before participating and speak privately with the instructor about reasonable modifications. Instructors do not diagnose or provide medical treatment.

3. Consent and respectful instruction

Instructors should explain activities, offer options, respect boundaries, and obtain permission before any hands-on adjustment, photography, or other personal contact. Participants may withdraw consent at any time. No participant should be pressured to disclose personal, medical, spiritual, or trauma-related information.

4. Accessibility and inclusion

The school aims to create welcoming learning environments across different bodies, ages, abilities, backgrounds, and levels of experience. Participants are encouraged to communicate access needs in advance where possible. The school will work with hosts and instructors to identify reasonable adaptations and clarify any site-specific limitations.

5. Community conduct

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted contact, retaliation, and disrespect for another person's boundaries are not acceptable. Follow the host location's safety rules, respect shared spaces, and treat participants, instructors, volunteers, and neighbors with care. Concerns may be raised with the instructor, program coordinator, host contact, or school administration.

6. Emergencies and incidents

If there is an immediate threat to life or safety, call 911. Notify the instructor or host-site contact as soon as it is safe to do so. Instructors and program leads should follow the location's emergency procedures, seek appropriate assistance, and document incidents through the school's designated process. Program organizers must confirm emergency contacts, exits, first-aid resources, and site-specific procedures before each activity.

7. Minors and safeguarding

Programs involving minors require advance approval, parent or guardian consent, clear supervision responsibilities, appropriate instructor screening where required, and defined check-in and release procedures. A minor should not be enrolled through an adult-only registration process.

8. Photography and media

Photography, audio, or video for public communications requires an appropriate consent process. Participation in a class should not be treated as permission to publish a participant's image, voice, name, or personal story. Separate consent should be obtained, and consent may be withdrawn for future use where practicable.

9. Program-specific requirements

Some activities, locations, or partner programs may require additional waivers, health and safety information, equipment instructions, or site rules. Those requirements should be provided before participation and should identify the responsible program operator and host.

10. Raise a concern

Questions or safety concerns may be sent to adminsensesyogaschool@gmail.com. If the concern involves immediate danger, use emergency services rather than waiting for an email response.
