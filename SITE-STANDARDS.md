# Senses Yoga School — Site Standards

Last updated: November 23, 2026

## Purpose
The website is a living public record of Senses Yoga School: its education, community relationships, service, stewardship, and developing work. It should help visitors understand the school and find a genuine next step without overstating capacity or outcomes.

## Shared navigation
Use the same six primary links on every public page: Discover (index.html), Academics (schools.html), Library (library.html), Campus (calendar.html), Impact (impact.html), Support (support.html). The shared site-navigation.js file is the header navigation source. Keep footer links consistent.

## Three participation pathways
- Learning: structured programs, practice, study, mentorship, and approved apprenticeship learning.
- Connection: informal community activities, including chess gatherings. These are for belonging and interaction, not academic credit.
- Service: volunteer contribution and stewardship, including Gathering Table and Darius Simmons Garden.

Do not merge these categories into one undifferentiated list. Volunteer service is not automatically an academic course or apprenticeship placement. A field placement requires explicit approval, a defined learning purpose, supervision, and appropriate records.

## Four Schools of Practice
- School of Wellness
- School of Ecology / Prakṛti
- School of Specialized Studies
- School of Nāṭyaśāstra & Creative Arts / MOGA

Describe them as connected disciplines within one school. State whether each program is active, a past pilot, developing, or proposed.

## Page responsibilities
- Discover: explain the school and offer clear entry points.
- Academics: explain the four Schools of Practice and formal learning pathways.
- Library: organize study resources and source attribution.
- Campus: show public events and Learning / Connection / Service pathways.
- Impact: report documented work with dates, methods, and limitations.
- Support: explain financial and in-kind support pathways.
- Partnerships: explain collaboration, hosting, introductions, and institutional relationships.
- Volunteer: explain intake, supervision, service records, and service opportunities.
- Serve: explain the contribution model and connect people to ways to serve.
- Practice: help visitors find public practice and at-home resources.

## Reporting and claims
Never invent attendance, outcomes, hours, funding, or partner commitments. Preserve reporting periods for historical numbers. Separate engagements from unique people. Describe self-reported wellness results as self-reported, not clinical evidence. Mark proposals and developing work clearly. Confirm partner names, roles, permissions, and event details before publishing.

## Design and accessibility
Use sun-yellow / gold as primary, purple as secondary, and green as tertiary. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image alt text, semantic headings, and responsive mobile layouts. Avoid unnecessary cards, duplicated navigation, and repeated introductions. Keep links descriptive and verify calls to action.

## Maintenance
After changing a public route, update the sitemap. After changing a program, verify related Academics, Campus, Impact, Volunteer, Serve, and Support pages. Test the deployed site on mobile and desktop; a GitHub commit alone does not verify production rendering.
