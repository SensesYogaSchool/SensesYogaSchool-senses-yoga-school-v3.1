About the name

Saṅgha — community in practice.

Saṅgha is a Sanskrit word meaning assembly or community, especially associated with Buddhist traditions and also used more broadly in Indic contexts. We use it as an intentional name for shared learning, not as a literal ancient translation of a modern recreational club. Club gatherings are separate from approved apprenticeship field placements.
