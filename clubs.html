Chess Gatherings

A simple way to meet, play, and connect.

Chess is a weekly community activity—not an academic course or apprenticeship requirement. Students, apprentices, educators, and friends can meet across the board for informal games, friendly interaction, and shared enjoyment with Bradford Beach Chess.

There are no academic expectations or skill prerequisites. Check the calendar for the current gathering time and location before attending.

About the name

Saṅgha — community in practice.

Saṅgha is a Sanskrit word meaning assembly or community, especially associated with Buddhist traditions and also used more broadly in Indic contexts. We use it as an intentional name for community connection. These activities are informal opportunities to build relationships; they are not academic courses or approved apprenticeship field placements.
