## Topic categories

- **Ecology and reciprocal stewardship:** reciprocity, responsibility, restoration, and relationships with land.
- **Indigenous ecological knowledge:** respectful study of Indigenous perspectives alongside scientific inquiry, with attribution and without claiming to represent all Indigenous traditions.
- **Botany and plant relationships:** observation, plant life, seasonal cycles, and attentive learning.
- **Gift economies and gratitude:** gifts, giving back, and community food systems.
- **Environmental ethics:** sustainable harvesting, care, and accountability.
- **Community learning and service:** ecological education through shared work and reflection.

## YAGI educational applications

**Garden Park stewardship:** Connect garden observations, planting, composting, harvesting, and resource-sharing with questions of reciprocity and ecological responsibility.

**Foodways:** Reflect on the relationships among growers, donors, preparers, eaters, soil, and plants; avoid reducing the book to recipes or unverified claims about plant medicine.

**Apprenticeship:** Pair field notes and site observations with discussions of ethical harvest, gratitude, and the limits of taking from a shared ecosystem.
