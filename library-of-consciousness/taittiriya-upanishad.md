# Taittirīya Upaniṣad — Library of Consciousness

**Collection:** Senses Yoga School — Library of Consciousness  
**Status:** Source catalog entry; translation and quotations pending verification  
**Tradition:** Vedic / Upaniṣadic literature; associated with the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda  
**Primary topics:** Vedānta; food and nourishment (*anna*); five sheaths (*pañca kośa*); consciousness and Self-inquiry; five elements; ecology and interdependence; ethical hospitality.

## Topic pathways

- **Food, nourishment and ecology:** *Bṛghuvallī* (Book 3) explores food and the interdependence of life. Relevant to YAGI Foodways and shared meals.
- **Five kośas and consciousness:** *Brahmānandavallī* (Book 2) presents a progressive contemplation of the food, vital, mental, discernment and bliss sheaths. Relevant to contemplative study and embodiment.
- **Ethics and hospitality:** *Śikṣāvallī* (Book 1), including the convocation teaching, supports reflection on truthfulness, learning, giving and honoring guests.
- **Five elements:** Use as a comparative thematic connection with broader Vedāntic cosmology; avoid presenting every modern chakra-element mapping as a direct claim of this text.

## Editorial practice

The Taittirīya Upaniṣad is an ancient source, but modern English translations may be copyrighted. Before adding direct quotes to the website or cookbook, identify the edition, translator, section and publication status. Do not attribute paraphrases as verbatim quotations. Distinguish the original text's teachings from Senses Yoga School's modern educational interpretation.
