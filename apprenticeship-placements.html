Applied Sevā Standard

100 hours. Supervised. Documented.

The Semester II framework includes a supervised 100-hour service-learning standard. It also requires 17 portfolio submissions, pathway-specific competency evidence, learner reflection, and mentor or supervisor review. This is the approved academic framework, not a claim that all volunteer time automatically earns credit.

Individual placements, schedules, supervision, partner agreements, and funding remain in development until confirmed. Public volunteering is available separately from the apprenticeship.

How It Works

Study → Practice → Direct Experience → Observation → Reflection → Application → Continued Study.

Students first complete the required preparation, agree on a suitable supervised placement, document approved service, and return to reflection and review. Pathway availability depends on active projects and mentors.
