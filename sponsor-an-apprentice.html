Education in Practice

Learning that returns to the neighborhood.

The Heroic Yogi Apprenticeship joins contemplative practice, guided study, mentorship, and applied community service.

Semester I — Sādhaka, The Dedicated Practitioner: a 15-week foundation in personal practice, yoga study, reflection, and supervised learning.

Semester II — Vīrayogī, The Heroic Yogi: a 9–12-week applied specialization pathway with a 100-hour Applied Sevā & Service-Learning Standard, including opportunities across wellness, ecology, Nāda, creative arts, and specialized studies.

Applicants enter through a readiness and orientation process. Sponsorship does not confer admission, employment, credentials, or guaranteed placement.

Ways to Partner

Three ways to make education possible.

Individual Apprentice Sponsor

Support one apprentice's educational access, instruction, mentorship, learning materials, and supervised practice. Funding scope and cost are agreed in a written sponsorship proposal.

Founding Cohort Sponsor

Help underwrite the limited three-student winter cohort, or contribute toward a future spring cohort. Sponsors may support a portion or the full approved cohort budget.

In-Kind Education Partner

Offer instructional space, supplies, printing, accessibility resources, transportation support, mentorship, or other documented resources that reduce delivery costs.

Seasonal Priorities

Start small. Document well. Grow responsibly.

Winter 2026–2027 · Founding cohort

Up to three students total. Our immediate sponsorship priority is to secure educational support for this small cohort, confirm each learner's funding position, and document outcomes responsibly.

Spring 2027 · Planned expansion

A larger cohort of approximately six to eight students is being explored, subject to educator capacity, community-site readiness, applicant review, and sponsorship commitments.

Seasonal plans are targets, not guaranteed enrollment or funded openings.

Winter 2026–2027 · Illustrative Sponsorship Target

Build a foundational library. Sustain the mentor.

$2,090 per apprentice · $6,270 for three

Proposed per-apprentice allocation: instruction and supervised learning (45 attributable hours × $30 = $1,350); foundational books, workbook, supplies, and acquisition reserve ($250); coordination and learner support ($300); contingency ($190).

The materials allowance includes a proposed hardcover copy of Sir Edwin Arnold’s The Song Celestial, other Semester I foundational texts, printing, and study supplies. The exact editions and purchase prices will be verified before ordering.

Planning estimate only. This is not tuition charged to an apprentice or a final approved delivery budget. Cohort-sharing efficiencies, donated resources, actual mentor hours, and funding arrangements may change the amount. Semester II materials are budgeted separately.

Responsible Stewardship

What a sponsorship supports—and how we report back.

Sponsorship budgets may include educator time, supervision, educational materials, learning-site access, program administration, and the costs of applied community service. Each proposal will specify the agreed scope, contribution method, and reporting expectations.

We aim to share appropriate aggregate measures such as participation, learning milestones, supervised activities, and service outputs while respecting apprentice privacy and consent.

Voluntary donations or in-kind contributions are never prerequisites for an applicant's admission. We do not describe a contribution as tax-deductible unless the receiving entity and arrangement have been verified.
