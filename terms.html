A straightforward guide to using Senses Yoga School's website, educational resources, and program information.

Users and participants are expected to communicate respectfully, respect privacy and consent, follow host-site rules, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disruptive conduct. Program-specific community standards may provide additional requirements.

The website is provided on an as-available basis. To the extent permitted by law, the school makes no guarantee that the website will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for every purpose. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded.

These terms may be updated as the school and its services develop. Questions about the website may be sent to adminsensesyogaschool@gmail.com.
