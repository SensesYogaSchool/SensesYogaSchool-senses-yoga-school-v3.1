Community Foodways · September 19, 2026

43 neighbors nourished at the health fair.

At the Medical College of Wisconsin Saturday Clinic health fair, Senses Yoga School's Foodways contributors helped provide food for 43 people. The effort brought together donated produce, culturally rooted cooking, and practical nutrition education.

Foodways contributors included Ibrahim Khalil Labib Jafuneh, Andrew Jiang, Imaad Said, and Luan Seguim-Arnold, with additional cooking assistance from the team. Produce support came from Gathering Table.

The educational materials explored accessible, affordable food substitutions and the DASH approach to heart-healthy eating. The recipes below are preserved from the shared educational Canva; the ingredient lists may differ from the exact portions prepared on event day.

School of Prakṛti · Living Foodways Archive

Recipes shared through community service.

The first entries come from Imaad Said's DASH Diet Education Canva for the September 2026 health fair. These are ingredient guides for eight servings; full preparation methods are being documented with contributors.

01 · Vegetarian · 8 servings

Vegetable Benachin

A vegetable-centered version of benachin, highlighting rice, seasonal produce, herbs, and accessible substitutions.

View ingredients
  • 3 cups dry long-grain rice; 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 4 tomatoes; 2 bell peppers; 1 zucchini; 1 yellow squash
  • 8 ounces mushrooms; 2 potatoes; 2 sweet potatoes
  • 1 bunch radishes; 2 cooked or roasted beets; 1 head lettuce
  • 2 bouillon cubes dissolved in 4 cups water
  • ½ cup parsley; ¼ cup basil; 1 teaspoon salt; black pepper to taste
  • Optional to serve: bread and cheese

Preparation steps pending contributor review. Check bouillon ingredients for dietary needs.

02 · Vegan · 8 servings

Pasta with Lentil Ragout

Whole-wheat pasta with lentils, mushrooms, vegetables, tomato sauce, and herbs.

View ingredients
  • 6 quarts water; 1 cup brown lentils; 16 ounces whole-wheat pasta
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil; 1 onion; 1 large celery stalk; ½ red bell pepper
  • 8 ounces white mushrooms; 2 garlic cloves
  • One 15-ounce can no-salt-added tomato sauce; 1 teaspoon low-sodium soy sauce; ¼ cup water
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning; 1 teaspoon low/no-sodium spice blend
  • 1–2 teaspoons lemon juice; black pepper to taste

Preparation steps pending contributor review. Contains wheat and soy unless substituted.

Source and credits: DASH Diet Education, shared by Imaad Said. Foodways contributors credited in the original educational material: Luan Seguim-Arnold, Imaad Said, Ibrahim Khalil Labib Jafuneh, and Andrew Jiang. Recipes are shared as educational resources, not individualized medical advice.

YAGI · School of Ecology

A living curriculum.

YAGI is the living, place-based expression of the School of Ecology, integrating ecological literacy, stewardship, foodways, yoga, creative practice, observation, workshops, and community leadership.

Place-Based Learning

The site is part of the curriculum.

Students and community members learn through observation, stewardship, practice, foodways, ecological care, documentation, and the changing conditions of a real place.
