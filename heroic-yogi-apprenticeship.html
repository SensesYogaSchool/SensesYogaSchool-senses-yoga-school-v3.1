The Educational Path

Practice is where the path begins.

The Heroic Yogi Apprenticeship invites thoughtful learners to develop an enduring relationship with traditional yoga, contemplative inquiry, ecological awareness, and service.

Students study with the Library of Consciousness, cultivate sādhana (साधना)—personal spiritual practice—and learn through guided mentorship and reflective documentation. Prior teaching credentials are not required.

Three intertwined commitments
01 · Adhyayana · अध्ययन

Study & Inquiry

Yoga philosophy, reading, Sanskrit study where appropriate, dialogue, and careful reflection.

02 · Sādhana · साधना

Practice & Formation

Embodied practice, observation, personal discipline, and mentorship grounded in lived experience.

03 · Sevā · सेवा

Service & Stewardship

Community wellness, foodways, ecological work, and supervised field learning.

The Community Is Our Campus

Learning belongs in the world.

Our school brings education into community spaces and ecological settings across Milwaukee. Apprenticeship field placements are supervised and approved individually; prospective partnerships are not guaranteed placements.

Semester II includes a 100-hour Applied Sevā and Service-Learning Standard, with documented learning and mentor review.
