The Educational Path

Practice is where the path begins.

The Heroic Yogi journey began with my own questions about practice and how to carry what I was learning into everyday life. Today, that journey is becoming a shared apprenticeship. We are still growing as a founder- and volunteer-led school, learning from teachers, neighbors, community partners, and one another.

We return to our books in the Library of Consciousness, develop sādhana (साधना)—personal practice—and keep asking what our experiences are teaching us. We observe, write, serve, and come back to practice. You do not need teaching credentials to begin.

The Community Is Our Campus

Learning belongs in the world.

Our classrooms are already appearing in the places where we practice and serve across Milwaukee. We are grateful to the people who welcome us, share what they know, and help this school take root. Apprenticeship field placements are supervised and approved individually; prospective partnerships are not guaranteed placements.
