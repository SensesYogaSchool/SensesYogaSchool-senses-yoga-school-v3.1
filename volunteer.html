The digital pathway

Simple to enter. Clear to sustain.

One public intake begins the process. Formal documents and verified records are used only when a placement calls for them.

  1. 1
    Submit the Volunteer Interest Form

    Share your interests, availability, and relevant experience through the school’s public intake.

  2. 2
    Staff review and placement

    The response enters the school’s volunteer record. A staff member follows up to discuss a current need, supervising lead, service-hour requirement when applicable, and appropriate next step.

  3. 3
    Confirm the working relationship

    Ongoing, partner-site, minor, academic, or civic-service placements may include a formal agreement before work begins.

  4. 4
    Track approved service in Jibble

    After placement, the school provides the time-tracking process. Jibble is the school’s official record for verified service hours.

  5. 5
    Complete and verify

    A supervising lead reviews the record. Completion or service documentation is issued from verified Jibble hours and placement records.

Community service

Need hours verified?

Tell us at the beginning.

The intake asks for your required hours, deadline, and school, court, or community program—without asking for case details. We will review whether a suitable supervised placement is available before promising hours or documentation.

Hours completed before an approved placement or outside the Jibble process cannot be retroactively certified.

What you receive
  • A defined placement and supervising contact
  • Orientation appropriate to the role and site
  • Jibble service-hour tracking after approval
  • Verified completion documentation when requirements are met
