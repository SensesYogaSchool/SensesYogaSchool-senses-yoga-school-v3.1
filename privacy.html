Privacy & Data Practices

How Senses Yoga School approaches information shared through its website, registration forms, and community programs.

Working draft · November 2026
Publication review required. This working draft reflects the services currently identified by the school: GitHub for website development and GitHub Pages hosting, a custom domain originally registered through Google Domains, Typeform for program forms, and Givebutter for online payments. The school must still verify account settings, access permissions, analytics, retention practices, and any additional service providers before adopting this as its final policy.

1. Who this notice covers

This notice applies to information submitted through the Senses Yoga School website and forms linked from it, including program interest, registration, attendance, volunteer, and participant feedback forms. Different programs may be administered by different legal entities or community partners. Where a registration or agreement identifies a specific operator or host, that notice or agreement also applies.

The nonprofit educational organization is currently in development. It has an EIN but has not yet completed Wisconsin incorporation or received IRS recognition as a 501(c)(3) organization. This notice does not represent it as an existing tax-exempt organization.

2. Information you may choose to provide

Depending on the form or program, you may provide contact details, program interests, registration and attendance information, volunteer or apprenticeship interests, feedback, and information you choose to share when communicating with the school. Please do not submit Social Security numbers, payment-card details, passwords, or detailed medical records through general interest or registration forms.

3. How information is used

  • To respond to questions and program inquiries.
  • To coordinate registrations, schedules, attendance, and program communications.
  • To administer volunteer, apprenticeship, and community learning opportunities.
  • To evaluate and improve educational programming and report appropriately on program outcomes.
  • To maintain safety and respond to incidents or urgent concerns when necessary.

4. Website hosting, forms, and payment services

The school maintains its website files in a GitHub repository and the site is configured with the custom domain www.sensesyoga.org. The repository includes a custom-domain configuration file consistent with GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Review GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. The school does not control GitHub's independent security logging.

The domain was originally registered through Google Domains. Google Domains registrations were migrated to Squarespace Domains; domain registration and DNS management are separate from the website's hosting. Review Squarespace's migration information.

The website currently links to forms hosted by Typeform, including forms for session registration, check-in, check-out, and volunteer interest. When you use a Typeform form, information is submitted through Typeform's service and may be processed under its own privacy terms as well as the settings and account arrangements configured by the school. School administrators have access to form responses, which are organized in Google Sheets for program administration and recordkeeping. Access to those spreadsheets should be limited to authorized administrators and others who need the information for an assigned school responsibility. The school is reviewing retention and sharing settings and will update this notice if its practices change. Please review Typeform's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

The school uses Givebutter for online payments and fundraising. When you follow a Givebutter payment or campaign link, information you enter is collected through Givebutter and its payment-processing providers. Depending on the campaign and the information you choose to provide, the school or campaign organizer may receive information such as your name, contact details, contribution amount, transaction details, and campaign responses. Givebutter states that payment-card and bank-account credentials are collected and processed by its payment processor rather than stored directly by Givebutter. Review Givebutter's Privacy Policy before making a payment. Givebutter campaign proceeds are paid to the SENSES YOGA LLC business bank account held at Relay. The school may also use Relay to maintain its business banking records. Relay's own privacy terms govern information it processes in connection with the bank account. The school should update this notice if a campaign's recipient or banking arrangement changes.

The school also links to an external Google Calendar for public scheduling. When you open an external service, that service's privacy terms apply to its handling of your information. We will update this notice if additional analytics, payment, mailing, or other service providers are introduced.

5. Sharing and access

Information may be accessed by authorized school administrators and, where needed to deliver a program, relevant instructors, program coordinators, or hosting partners. The school should limit access to people who need the information for the stated purpose. We do not describe information as being shared with a partner unless that sharing is necessary for the program and appropriately disclosed.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for program administration, safety, legal obligations, and responsible recordkeeping. Actual retention periods and deletion practices depend on the systems used and must be confirmed by the school. At least once each year, an authorized administrator should review access to Typeform accounts and connected Google Sheets, confirm that sharing remains restricted to people with an active school responsibility, remove access that is no longer needed, and review whether older records can be securely deleted or anonymized. This review should also take place when an administrator, instructor, or program partner leaves a role or no longer needs access. The school uses reasonable administrative practices to limit access, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and questions

You may contact the school to ask about information you submitted, request correction, or ask that information no longer be used for optional communications. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, safety, or legal reasons. Requests can be sent to adminsensesyogaschool@gmail.com.

8. Children and sensitive information

Parents or guardians should contact the school before submitting information for a child or arranging a minor's participation. Program-specific consent and supervision requirements must be established before a minor participates. Do not submit sensitive health information through general website forms.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice will be revised when the school's legal structure, services, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date and responsible legal entity should be confirmed before this page is published as a final policy.

Current payment arrangement: Online payments are collected through Givebutter for SENSES YOGA LLC. Campaign proceeds are paid to the LLC's Relay business bank account. The nonprofit educational arm is not currently the recipient of these payments.
