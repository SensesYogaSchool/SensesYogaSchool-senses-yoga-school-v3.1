# School page standard

All four Schools of Practice share this structure while retaining school-specific offerings and status:

1. 420px desktop / 320px mobile banner, subtle gradient, readable title over image; introduction and actions below.
2. Photographed entry points or offerings with clear descriptions. Label contextual artwork and archive images; do not imply that a photograph documents an offering it does not show.
3. Ways to contribute through participation, service, or collaboration, linked to existing intake, calendar, or partnership routes.
4. Community story with credited photography showing how participation sustains the school.
5. Preserve distinctions between current offerings, developing projects, volunteering, and individually approved apprenticeship placements.

Shared presentation: school-pages.css. Prakriti also uses health-fair-gallery.css for its gallery and service-area detail.
