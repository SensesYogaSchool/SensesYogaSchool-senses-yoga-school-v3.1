Shared stewardship · Community is our campus

Free learning has real value.

Senses Yoga School is currently founder-led. Its educational work grows through sustained dedication and meaningful community relationships. We are seeking in-kind administrative expertise, grant development support, sponsorships, and partners to help sustain accessible learning.

Community relationships in practice

  • Sherman Phoenix: Fiscal sponsorship; sponsorship of eight weeks of Yoga for Life; and 20 weeks of in-kind hosting for Thursday evening Yoga for Life sessions, 5:30–6:30 PM.
  • Neighborhood House: Landscaping tool-sharing arrangement supporting Garden Park stewardship.
  • All Peoples Church — Darius Simmons Garden: Educational mentorship and garden site access.
  • All Peoples Church — Gathering Table: Foodways and community service-learning opportunities.
  • Milwaukee Public Library: Free community spaces for planning, meetings, and live educational demonstrations. An annual, free Yoga for Life program for students and community members is being developed as a proposal, not an approved offering.
  • Near West Side Partners: Developing institutional relationship; future roles remain exploratory.

Ways to help sustain the work

We welcome administrative and financial stewardship, grant-readiness assistance, donated professional services, materials, volunteer labor, sponsorships, and partner introductions. Current Garden Park fundraising priorities include a shed and rainwater collection build and installation. Proposed 2027 Phase II Community Improvement Project improvements remain in planning and subject to funding and approvals.

Stronger administrative systems and collaborative capacity can support applications for eligible private and public grants; funding is not guaranteed.

Senses Yoga School's community campus is rooted in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, through practical relationships supporting free learning, service, wellness, and ecological stewardship. We are also cultivating relationships connected to service work, sustainable communities, and Indigenous stewardship in Mexico and Uganda. These international connections are developing; they are not presented as confirmed institutional partnerships.
