Partner

Build the classroom together.

Senses works with organizations that want accessible, place-responsive wellness, education, ecology, arts, workforce, or leadership programming where people already gather.

Community Organizations
Neighborhood, faith-based, nonprofit, and grassroots partnerships.

Aging & Accessible Wellness
Adaptive opportunities shaped around older adults, disability access, mobility, breath, and belonging.

Schools & Youth Programs
Movement, ecology, reflection, creative learning, service, and leadership development.

Parks & Public Space
Seasonal public practice, recreation, garden learning, and place-based community education.

Universities
Service learning, internships, field experience, workshops, research, and community-engaged education.

Workplaces & Workforce
Professional wellness plus developing ecology-based training pathways that connect supervised learning, practical competency, and employment partners.

Ways to Collaborate

Start with a real community need.

Host

Bring Yoga For Life, Heroic Breathwork, ecology education, workshops, or another Senses offering into your setting.

Build

Co-design a recurring program, public-space activation, service-learning experience, workforce pathway, or community project.

Sponsor

Help keep community participation accessible by supporting programs, materials, learning sites, apprentices, or training opportunities.
