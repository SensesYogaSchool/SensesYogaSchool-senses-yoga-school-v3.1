Relationships & Partnerships

We build through relationship.

Senses Yoga School is a decentralized community of practice. Host sites, collaborators, artists, fiscal support, neighborhood relationships, educators, and community participation help the school become real in public life.

We distinguish active relationships from developing ones. The goal is not to display a wall of logos. It is to show how people and organizations actually help hold the ecosystem.

Ways We Build Together

Partnership can take different forms.

A relationship may begin with a single class or conversation and grow through repeated work. We prefer clear, useful collaborations over inflated partnership language.

Host
Bring a Senses practice, workshop, learning series, or community program into a place where people already gather.

Refer
Connect residents, students, families, workers, or community members with accessible opportunities to practice and learn.

Sponsor Access
Help keep community programming affordable or free while sustaining the educators and materials required to deliver it.

Collaborate
Develop ecology, arts, wellness, educational, research, service-learning, or community projects together.

Volunteer & Steward
Participate in learning sites, events, garden stewardship, resource sharing, documentation, or community service.

Support Educator Capacity
Invest in apprentices, teachers, coordinators, and the human infrastructure that keeps community education consistent.
