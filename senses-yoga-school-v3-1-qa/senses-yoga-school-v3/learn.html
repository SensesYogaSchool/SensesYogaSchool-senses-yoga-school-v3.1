Learn

Practice becomes inquiry.

Senses approaches yoga as a living field of education—embodied, contemplative, interdisciplinary, and developed through sustained practice rather than information alone.

Educational Foundation

Heroic Yogi

The common developmental foundation connecting embodied practice, contemplative study, story, consciousness, self-inquiry, and service. Apprentices meet yogic teachings not only as concepts, but through archetypal lenses that ask how wisdom becomes lived character.

Schools of Thought

Distinct lenses through which students encounter practice, philosophy, relationship, ecology, creativity, and consciousness.

Vedic Arts

Movement, sound, storytelling, literary arts, sacred image, ritual imagination, and embodied forms of learning approached with context and care.

School of Practice

Specialized Studies

Focused explorations—including Sanskrit, advanced yoga philosophy, Dreamscaping, mythology, contemplative psychology, research, and other evolving areas of study.

Heroic Yogi Apprenticeship

The archetypes are companions on the path.

The apprenticeship uses figures and stories from yogic traditions as lenses for practice, inquiry, and service. They are studied in historical and religious context, then carried into reflection with care—not treated as costumes or personality labels.

Gaṇeśa · The Threshold

Begin with attention.

Semester One follows the Gaṇeśa Path: foundations of yogic practice, teaching, and leadership. The apprentice encounters tradition, establishes sādhana, learns to observe, and meets obstacles as places for discernment.

Hanumān · Shared Archetypal Research

Strength bows. Devotion serves.

Hanumān offers an archetypal lens for courage, focus, humility, and service—strength placed in relationship with something greater than the self.

This interface image was created as concept research for the dream-based game Dream Master. It demonstrates how an archetype might become an interactive relationship rather than a collectible character; it is not a finished apprenticeship screen.

Rāmāyaṇa · The Living Story

Let story test the practice.

As the apprenticeship develops, the Rāmāyaṇa becomes a narrative field for studying dharma, exile, relationship, devotion, discernment, courage, and responsible action.

Traditional artworks: early nineteenth-century Gaṇeśa miniature and an eighteenth-century Rāmāyaṇa painting, via Wikimedia Commons. Hanumān interface: Dream Master visual-concept research, co-created within Senses Yoga School.

A Vedic Field of Learning

Tradition is encountered through practice.

Students meet yogic and Vedic thought through embodied inquiry. Texts, stories, Sanskrit seeds, archetypal images, contemplative arts, and philosophies of consciousness are studied alongside the realities of body, relationship, land, and service.

The school does not ask learners to abandon discernment. It asks them to deepen it.
