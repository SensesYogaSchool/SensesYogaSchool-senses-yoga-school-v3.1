About Senses · Living History

A school taking root in public life.

Senses Yoga School is a decentralized community school rooted in yoga and expressed through practice, inquiry, ecology, relationship, and service.

Its story is not contained in one building. It is visible in the places people gather, the practices they return to, the gardens they tend, and the relationships that slowly become a school.

Chapter III · Tradition

We inherit questions as well as practices.

Senses Yoga School is rooted in the traditions of yoga and informed by Vedic philosophy and arts. That inheritance is approached with reverence, inquiry, and responsibility rather than as surface decoration.

Traditional teachings meet lived experience through embodied practice, reflection, dialogue, creative expression, ecology, and service. The aim is not to imitate another time. It is to practice carefully enough that wisdom can become ethical action now.

Chapter IV · The Founder

Luan Seguim-Arnold

Founder & Executive Director

Luan’s journey began across cultures—born in Brazil and raised in the United States—and was shaped through the arts, community and environmental work, and sustained self-practice.

His path into yoga deepened through study and relationship with teachers connected to Rishikesh, India, and Milwaukee. Rather than separating yoga from ordinary life, his work increasingly asked how contemplative practice could become community education, ecological stewardship, service, and leadership.

Senses Yoga School grew from that question. The founder remains part of the journal, but not its only author: students, stewards, teachers, partners, neighbors, gardens, weather, and community life continue writing the school with him.

Chapter VI · An Evolving Ecosystem

The school is still becoming.

Today Senses lives through community partnerships, public practice, parks, gardens, educational programs, professional settings, and shared leadership across Milwaukee.

A sanctuary-centered home remains a longer-term direction, but the present school is already alive wherever its practices and relationships are being cared for.
