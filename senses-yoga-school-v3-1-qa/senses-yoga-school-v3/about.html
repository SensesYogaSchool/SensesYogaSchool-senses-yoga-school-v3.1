About Senses

Our Community Is Our Campus.

Senses Yoga School is a decentralized, community-centered educational ecosystem. Its mission is to cultivate individuals and communities through contemplative education rooted in the traditions of yoga and expressed through practice, inquiry, relationship, and service.

The Community-as-Campus Model

Education beyond four walls.

A classroom can be a garden. A park can become a place of inquiry. A yoga mat can become a laboratory for self-awareness. A community can become a school.
Our Relationship to Tradition

We inherit questions as well as practices.

Senses Yoga School is rooted in the traditions of yoga and informed by Vedic philosophy and arts. We approach that inheritance with reverence, inquiry, and responsibility rather than using it as surface decoration.

Traditional teachings meet lived experience through embodied practice, reflection, dialogue, creative expression, ecology, and service. The aim is not to imitate another time. It is to practice carefully enough that wisdom can become ethical action now.

A Decentralized School

Present ecosystem and long-term vision

Today, the school lives through community partnerships, public practice, parks, gardens, educational programs, and shared leadership across Milwaukee. A sanctuary-centered space remains a longer-term direction—not a facility the school currently claims to possess.
