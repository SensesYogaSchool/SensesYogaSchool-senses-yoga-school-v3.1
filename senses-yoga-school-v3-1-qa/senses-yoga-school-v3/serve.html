YAGI · School of Ecology

A living curriculum.

YAGI is the living, place-based expression of the School of Ecology, integrating ecological literacy, stewardship, foodways, yoga, creative practice, observation, workshops, and community leadership.

Place-Based Learning

The site is part of the curriculum.

Students and community members learn through observation, stewardship, practice, foodways, ecological care, documentation, and the changing conditions of a real place.
