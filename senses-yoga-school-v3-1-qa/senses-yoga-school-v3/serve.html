Serve · School of Ecology

Learn by caring for a real place.

Ecology at Senses is not only studied. It is practiced through stewardship, foodways, observation, community relationships, land care, and the skills required to sustain shared environments.

YAGI · Yoga Arts Garden Initiative

A living outdoor campus.

YAGI is the place-based learning environment of the School of Ecology, integrating ecological literacy, garden stewardship, foodways, yoga, creative practice, observation, workshops, and community leadership.

Place-Based Learning

The site is part of the curriculum.

Students, volunteers, and community members learn through the changing conditions of a real place: soil, plants, pests, weather, food, maintenance, neighbors, access, documentation, and shared care.

Ecology to Workforce

Stewardship can become a practical skill pathway.

The School of Ecology is developing supervised training pathways that connect ecological land-care principles with practical landscaping and workforce skills.

Our emerging collaboration model pairs Senses' community recruitment, ecological education, wellness, practice sites, and documentation with experienced trade partners who establish technical standards, evaluate competency, and determine employment placement. YAGI can serve as a learning environment without promising employment before a trainee demonstrates readiness.
