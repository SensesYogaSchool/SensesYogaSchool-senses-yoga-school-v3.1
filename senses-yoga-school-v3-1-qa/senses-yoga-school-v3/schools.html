Schools of Practice

The school as it exists now—and where it is growing.

Senses Yoga School is actively placing its educational work through three Schools of Practice. Additional Schools described in the Academic Catalog belong to the school's developing academic architecture and are planned for activation in 2027.

Active Schools · 2026

Current educational placements

Active · 2026

School of Community Wellness

Community-centered contemplative education, accessible practice, wellness literacy, Yoga For Life, and community educator development.

Active · 2026

School of Ecology

Ecological literacy, stewardship, foodways, place-based education, and YAGI as a living learning environment.

Active · 2026

School of Professional Wellness

Contemplative education applied to workplaces and professional communities through resilience, recovery, attention, mobility, and sustainable practice.

Across the Schools · Archetypal Development

Stories become mirrors for lived practice.

The Academic Catalog's archetypal learning environment is carried publicly through Heroic Yogi. Learners may encounter figures such as Ganesha, Hanuman, Rama and Sita, Krishna, Lakshmi, Durga, Bhūmi, Buddha, and Jesus through their distinct traditions—not as interchangeable beliefs, but as companions for inquiry into courage, devotion, discernment, relationship, stewardship, compassion, service, and human flourishing.
