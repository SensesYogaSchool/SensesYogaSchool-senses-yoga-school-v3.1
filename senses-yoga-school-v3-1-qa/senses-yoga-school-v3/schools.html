Schools of Practice

The school as it exists now—and where it is growing.

Senses Yoga School is actively placing its educational work through three Schools of Practice. Additional Schools described in the Academic Catalog belong to the school's developing academic architecture and are planned for activation in 2027.

Active Schools · 2026

Current educational placements

School of Community Wellness
Active. Community-centered contemplative education, accessible practice, wellness literacy, Yoga For Life, and community educator development.

School of Ecology
Active. Ecological literacy, stewardship, foodways, place-based education, and YAGI as a living learning environment.

School of Professional Wellness
Active. Contemplative education applied to workplaces and professional communities through resilience, recovery, attention, mobility, and sustainable practice.

Developing for 2027

Future academic activations

School of Specialized Studies

Planned for 2027. The Academic Catalog establishes the future framework for scholarship, independent inquiry, contemplative research, and focused areas of study.

School of Natyasāstra & Creative Arts

Planned for 2027. The Academic Catalog establishes the future framework for movement, sound, storytelling, literary and performing arts, creativity, and culture as contemplative practice.

Living Architecture

The catalog is a direction, not a claim that every School is already operating.

The public website distinguishes current placements from future academic development so partners and learners can see clearly what Senses offers today while understanding the educational ecosystem being built.
