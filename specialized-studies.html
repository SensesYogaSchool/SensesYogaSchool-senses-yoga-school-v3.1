Foundational Text · Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad

Four states. One inquiry into consciousness.

The Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad offers a concise framework for examining waking (jāgrat), dreaming (svapna), deep sleep (suṣupti), and turīya—the fourth, understood as the underlying reality beyond the three ordinary states. These teachings inform our study of dream experience, self-inquiry, and contemplative practice without reducing them to modern scientific claims.

01 · जाग्रत्

Waking

Awareness engaged with the outward world and everyday experience.

02 · स्वप्न

Dreaming

Experience within the dreaming mind and the inward field of images.

03 · सुषुप्ति

Deep Sleep

Study of dreamless sleep and the limits of ordinary knowing.

04 · तुरीय

Turīya

The fourth: not merely another dream state, but the nondual reality described by the text.

Classical contemplative interpretation is presented distinctly from empirical sleep science. Study includes the text, guided reflection, and comparative inquiry.
