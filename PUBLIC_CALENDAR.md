# Public calendar maintenance

The Campus page embeds the school's designated public Google Calendar. Only events intended for public participation belong on that calendar. Keep personal appointments, private partner planning, staff-only meetings, and confidential details off the public calendar.

## Event classification
- **Learning:** scheduled classes, courses, workshops, mentorship, and academic sessions.
- **Community activity:** informal connection and shared activities, such as weekly chess gatherings.
- **Service:** volunteer shifts and stewardship, including Gathering Table and Darius Simmons Garden.
- **Partner event:** an event hosted with an external organization and explicitly open to the public.

These labels describe the purpose of an event. A service shift is not automatically an academic course or an approved apprenticeship placement.

## Weekly review
1. Confirm public status, venue, date, start/end time, accessibility notes, and America/Chicago time zone.
2. Keep private appointments, partner planning, staff training, and confidential details off the public calendar.
3. Confirm public RSVP/check-in links and whether registration is required.
4. Verify recurring events remain active before retaining future occurrences.
5. Remove or update canceled events promptly.
6. Check the calendar embed on a phone after schedule changes.

Do not publish an event until its date, time, location, public status, and responsible contact are confirmed.
