Our Community Is Our Campus

Participation can deepen naturally.

A class can remain a class, or become a doorway into another practice, service, field study, the Heroic Yogi Apprenticeship, or community leadership. There is no requirement to move further than feels useful.

How the system works

Simple for participants. Organized for the school.

High-volume participation such as RSVPs and session check-ins belongs in structured records. Formal documents are reserved for deeper relationships such as volunteer placements, field study, apprenticeships, staff roles, and partner agreements.
