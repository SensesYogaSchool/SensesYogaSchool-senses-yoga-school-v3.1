What we are exploring

Svetta is a proposed learning and research initiative, not an installed energy network. The first step is to work with community members and qualified collaborators on a feasibility proposal and a small educational prototype.

Community energy

Investigate a solar-and-battery community node, practical energy monitoring, maintenance needs, and responsible financing.

Conscious technology

Explore an offline-first family interface, privacy-preserving monitoring, accessibility, and transparent community governance.

Ecological education

Bring energy literacy, local stewardship, resource responsibility, and community participation into an interdisciplinary learning pathway.

Early planning, not a promise of service

The working concept considers a sponsored community node with an initial planning target of up to 20 households, subject to feasibility, engineering, funding, community consent, and applicable regulation. No household energy service, free electricity, installation date, or sponsorship has been confirmed.

Safety and stewardship: Any real electrical connection would require qualified professionals, appropriate permits and utility coordination, technical safeguards, and separate approval. Digital communication connectors are not electrical infrastructure.

How the pilot may develop

01 · Listen and scope

Invite community perspectives and establish shared questions, boundaries, and decision-making.

02 · Test feasibility

Assess technical, environmental, legal, financial, privacy, and accessibility considerations.

03 · Prototype learning

Build a safe, small-scale educational demonstration and evaluate it before considering any deployment.

Help shape the proposal

We welcome conversation with neighborhood stewards, energy and battery specialists, licensed electricians, software and hardware developers, educators, accessibility advocates, legal and regulatory advisors, and prospective funding partners. Interest is an invitation to collaborate—not a commitment to build.
