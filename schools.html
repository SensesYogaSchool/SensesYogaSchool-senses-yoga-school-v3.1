How to Use the School

Start with a school. Move across the ecosystem.

A student may begin with a Yoga for Life class, serve at YAGI, study a text in the Library of Consciousness, or contribute to an arts project. The pathways are distinct, but they are not isolated.

Cross-School Pathways

Learning becomes practice, service, and leadership.

Heroic Yogi Apprenticeship, Dream Connections, community service, and partner projects can draw from more than one School of Practice. These pathways sit across the ecosystem rather than becoming additional departments.
