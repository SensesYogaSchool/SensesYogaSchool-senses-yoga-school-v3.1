## Learning ethics

Present symbolic practices as cultural, creative and contemplative inquiry rather than guaranteed predictions or clinical treatment. Preserve respect for the original traditions and sources; avoid claiming universal dream-symbol meanings. Student offerings should have appropriate instructor oversight and clear scope.
