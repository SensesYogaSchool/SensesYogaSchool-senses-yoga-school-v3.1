Organizational Structure & Accountability

A clear foundation for a decentralized ecosystem of education, community locations, and long-term stewardship.

Senses Yoga School is developing a hybrid organizational model intended to support both the practical work of sustaining community locations and the public-benefit work of accessible education. The model is designed around distinct legal responsibilities, transparent finances, and a shared commitment to community flourishing.

The organizational model

One shared mission. Distinct responsibilities.

The two arms are intended to complement one another without becoming interchangeable. Each must operate through its own legal authority, financial records, agreements, and accountability processes.

Existing legal entity

SENSES YOGA LLC

SENSES YOGA LLC is the existing Wisconsin domestic limited liability company associated with the school's developing commercial and operational model.

  • Community locations and related operating activity.
  • Real estate ownership, leasing, and property-related development as undertaken.
  • Commercial agreements, vendors, and operational expenses.
  • Business financing and credit activity undertaken in the LLC's own name.

Scope note: This describes the intended role of the LLC. It does not imply that the school currently owns a particular property or operates a permanent campus.

In development

Nonprofit educational arm

A separate nonprofit educational organization is being prepared to advance accessible learning and community benefit.

  • Community wellness and contemplative education.
  • Ecological stewardship and place-based learning.
  • Creative arts, cultural learning, and educational pathways.
  • Apprenticeship, leadership development, and eligible grant-supported programs.

Current status: An EIN has been obtained. Wisconsin state incorporation has not yet been completed, and the organization has not received IRS recognition as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization.

Shared purpose

Community is our campus.

The organizational model supports a decentralized ecosystem of education: learning in community rooms, gardens, parks, cultural spaces, and other places where people gather.

Our educational vision is grounded in accessible participation, reciprocal partnerships, ecological responsibility, and the understanding that knowledge carries a responsibility to be returned in service. The LLC and the developing nonprofit are intended to support different parts of this vision while preserving clear boundaries between commercial operations and charitable educational activity.

Accountability commitments

How we intend to protect clarity and trust.

01 · Separate records

Maintain distinct accounts, bookkeeping, budgets, contracts, and reporting for each legal entity.

02 · Written agreements

Document any shared facilities, staff, equipment, administrative services, or other transactions between the entities.

03 · Responsible governance

Establish nonprofit board oversight, conflict-of-interest procedures, and decisions that prioritize the nonprofit's own educational purposes.

Current legal-status notice. The nonprofit educational arm is in development. It has not yet completed Wisconsin incorporation and has not been recognized by the IRS as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3). Senses Yoga School does not currently represent contributions to this developing entity as tax-deductible charitable contributions.
