Contributions & Financial Transparency

Understand the difference between program fees, voluntary contributions, sponsorship, and future charitable giving.

Working draft · November 2026
Current status: The nonprofit educational arm is in development. It has an EIN, but Wisconsin incorporation and IRS 501(c)(3) recognition have not yet been completed. Payments made to the existing LLC or to the school at this stage are not represented as tax-deductible charitable contributions.

1. Clear payment descriptions

Depending on the activity, a payment to Senses Yoga School may be a program fee, a voluntary contribution toward operating costs, a payment for goods or services, or a sponsorship governed by a separate agreement. The payment page, invoice, receipt, or written agreement should identify the recipient and purpose.

2. Voluntary contributions

Some school activities are offered free or on a voluntary contribution basis. A contribution is not required to participate where a program is expressly offered without a required fee. Contributions received by the existing LLC are business receipts and are not charitable gifts to a 501(c)(3) organization.

3. Sponsorships and partnerships

Sponsorships may involve recognition, services, event support, or other agreed benefits. The terms should be documented in writing, including the receiving entity, amount, purpose, deliverables, and any recognition provided. A sponsorship payment is not automatically a charitable contribution.

4. Future nonprofit giving

If and when the nonprofit is legally incorporated and receives applicable IRS recognition, the school will identify the correct legal recipient and explain the status of eligible contributions. Until then, please do not treat payments to the school as tax-deductible charitable gifts.

5. Receipts and questions

For each payment, retain the receipt or written confirmation identifying the recipient. For questions about a payment or partnership, contact adminsensesyogaschool@gmail.com.

6. Transparency commitment

As the organizational structure develops, the school intends to publish clearer information about program funding, responsible entities, and the use of resources. Financial information will be shared in a manner consistent with legal obligations, privacy, and responsible stewardship.
